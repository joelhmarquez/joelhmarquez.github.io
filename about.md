I'm Joel Marquez, a Principal Engineering Manager at Microsoft. I lead a team of 9 engineers that owns the globally distributed services behind Microsoft Teams calls and meetings — systems that handle billions of requests a day at 99.99%+ availability across commercial, government, and sovereign clouds. Running real-time communications at that scale is the foundation of my work.

The newest layer on that foundation is AI. My team also owns the voice-agent platform behind Teams Phone's AI agents, and I led Intelligent Call Delegation — an AI agent that answers and handles calls on a user's behalf — from concept to preview, writing critical parts of its real-time voice path myself. The question driving my current work: how do agents become trustworthy enough for consequential work?

I joined Microsoft as a new-grad engineer in 2018 and reached Principal in 2026; I'm a co-inventor on 3 US patents. First-generation college graduate (computer science, University of Colorado Boulder), based in the Denver area. I also wrote this site's Sponsorship Handbook, a practical guide to employment-based permanent residency sponsorship. Outside of work I run — and lately I write: this site is where my essays live, mostly about agents, engineering leadership, and what I'm learning as I go.
